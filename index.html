✨ How It Works

So simple it almost feels like cheating. 😅

Three steps and your whole booking system runs itself. No tech skills needed — we handle everything.

1 📲

Client scans or taps

Every business gets a custom QR code and NFC tag. Customers scan at the counter — no app download needed, works from any phone in seconds.

2 🤖

AI books them instantly

Our AI assistant handles the whole conversation — checks availability, picks a time, confirms the booking. You don't lift a finger.

3 🎉

Reminders handle themselves

Automated follow-ups and no-show reminders go out automatically. Your calendar stays full, your no-show rate drops way down.
